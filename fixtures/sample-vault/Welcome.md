# Welcome to Amethyst

This vault is a small sky to explore. Every note is a star; every link is a line of light between them.

Start anywhere:

- [[Amethyst]] is the project that draws this map.
- [[Zettelkasten]] explains why linking notes beats filing them.
- [[Nebulae]] is where the astronomy cluster begins.
- [[Graph Theory]] is the math behind the picture.
- [[Ideas]] collects notes that don't exist yet.
- [[Writing in Amethyst]] shows every kind of formatting.

Press `Cmd+G` to step into the graph, and `Cmd+K` for every command.
