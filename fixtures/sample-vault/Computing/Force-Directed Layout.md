# Force-Directed Layout

Treat links as springs and nodes as charged particles that repel. Let the simulation cool, and clusters fall out on their own.

Barnes-Hut approximation makes repulsion O(n log n), one of the classic [[Algorithms]]. Positions stream to [[WebGL]] every frame. Theory lives in [[Graph Theory]].
