# Graph Theory

Vertices and edges, and everything you can say about them. A vault of linked notes is a directed graph.

- Drawing one legibly: [[Force-Directed Layout]].
- Finding its communities: [[Louvain Method]].
- Ranking its hubs: [[PageRank]].
- Walking it: breadth-first search from [[Algorithms]], stored with adjacency lists from [[Data Structures]].
