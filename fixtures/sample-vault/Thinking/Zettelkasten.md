# Zettelkasten

A slip-box of small, linked notes. The links, not the folders, carry the structure.

## Origins

Niklas Luhmann kept about 90,000 index cards and credited them as a conversation partner.

## Practice

- Write [[Atomic Notes]].
- Rewrite them into [[Evergreen Notes]].
- Connect everything: [[Linking Your Thinking]].
