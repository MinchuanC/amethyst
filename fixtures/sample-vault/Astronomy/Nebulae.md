# Nebulae

Clouds of hydrogen, helium, and dust. Some glow because young stars ionize them; others are dark against the stars behind.

![[crab-nebula.svg]]

The Crab Nebula is the remnant of a supernova seen in 1054. Dense pockets of gas inside nebulae collapse under gravity, which is how [[Star Formation]] begins.

Narrowband filters on [[Telescopes]] isolate single emission lines, which is why astrophotos look so saturated. Rendering that glow on a screen is a job for [[Shaders]].
